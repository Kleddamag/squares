# `s(206)` — open

Open. The best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s (T-056), gives
`s(206) ≤ 14.860158663395859`, and the strongest lower bound independently verified here
is `14.379088` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4811`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `14.860158663395861`, proved by the exact
rational certificate of Francisco Couzo’s packing
(`E-franciscouzo-2026-09-27-exact-replay`). It is **larger** than the best known
`14.860158663395859` two fields above it, by `2E-15`.

It is not the value of `s(206)` and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
side, `14.86015866339586012403…`, rounded up at the fifteen decimals the source prints.
The source writes each coordinate as a binary64 value, and rounded to rationals those
poses close only at a side 2 units of the last place above the printed one, so the
printed side itself is not certified here.
The `replay-failure` conflict and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter record
the difference. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Francisco Couzo’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md)
reports a packing of side `14.860158663395859` for this count, dated 26 September 2026
and unchanged at the pinned revision `f3c5a52` of 27 September 2026 (T-056). Its first
packing for this count, of side `14.860232206380317`, is dated 23 September 2026 at
16:25 UTC by the author’s clock and was committed again on 24 September 2026 at 10:34
UTC. The repository names no method and no tolerance; its author said on
[issue #227](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/227) that he found the packings
“with the help of Claude”.

This repository certifies it exactly.
The retained decimal pose rounds to an exact rational packing at centre dilation 1, of
side `14.8601586633958601240…`, `1.124e-15` above the printed side, and every pair and
every wall is decided over `ℚ` twice, by the promotion’s exact separating-axis test and
by an independent checker that shares no code with it
([receipt](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md#certified-here)).
That proves `s(206) ≤ 14.860158663395861`, the verified upper bound; it says nothing
about optimality.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
