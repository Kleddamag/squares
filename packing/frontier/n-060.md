Open. The best known packing gives $s(60) \le 8$, and the verified lower bound is

monotonicity, leaving a gap of $0.08$.

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 60$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{60}\rceil = 8$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

Nagamochi’s general closed form, the verified lower bound before this certificate,
applies to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
