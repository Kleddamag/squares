# Starting at the Low Side of a Sign-Definite Axis

If the derivative enclosure along $x$ is nonnegative over a box, the capture only grows
from the box’s left side, so $F(c) \ge F(x_{\text{left}}, \cdot)$ on that leg with no
penalty at all (lemma R8). At a box that fails the centre bound, evaluating the capture
at that side’s point and dropping the sign-definite axis’s penalty may certify it.

Prediction: 10 to 20% fewer boxes, at the cost of one more centre-value pass at failing
boxes with a sign-definite axis; at least 10% fewer search instructions.
