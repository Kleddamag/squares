### The Regime and the Decision

H-275’s claim is a question: “what fraction does the 17-owner kernel under SW9’s frozen
adaptive-row recipe exclude by a certificate the standing kernel verifier re-proves in
full, and at what producer, checker and verifier cost per state?”
Its regime fixes the 31 frozen states, “one run each, in the frozen run order on two
workers, until the run operator’s deadline at 2026-10-06T07:14:04Z”, the distance-2
draws “reported separately”, and “States not run by the deadline are reported as not
run, not extrapolated.”
It has `kind: open_question` and no `criterion` block, which the hypothesis schema
requires for `kind: hypothesis` and omits for open questions.

exp-257’s verdict reason:

> Accepted means the frozen measurement ran and answered H-275’s question for the draws
> it reached; it does not mean a falsifier survived, because H-275 is an open question
> and registers none.

> primary_criterion: At least half of the counted draws excluded within two CPU-hours
> each, with N1’s 7,000 s wall ceiling standing in for that limit; falsified by six
> non-closures of the ten counted.
> 
> reason: Five of the ten counted draws closed within the 7,000 s ceiling, each
> re-proved in full by the standing kernel verifier, which meets the criterion of at
> least half; …

H-264’s notes register that falsifier (“Affordability falsifier: fewer than half of the
sampled leaves excluded within 2 CPU-hours each … The criterion is met at five closures
and falsified at six non-closures”). exp-252’s acceptance therefore means a falsifier
did not fire. exp-257’s cannot mean that, and it says so.
exp-252 is not the like-for-like precedent.
The record does have one.

exp-252 and exp-257 among them.
exp-014 and exp-015 accept H-032 on exhaustive exact classifications, and eight of
exp-033 to exp-045 accept H-023 on preregistered determinations.

claims.
The ledger at `a0ff1cd9f` keeps H-275, H-264, H-023 and H-032 as “open question”,
so acceptance promotes nothing.

The verdict follows from the regime on the draws it reached, with two qualifications:
u31 was not run because of a rule the regime does not contain (F5), and the round

At the verdict, u31 had not run and u29, which closed at 07:06:36 inside the regime’s
07:14:04Z deadline, had no verification result.
The registration named two ways the round could end: the list exhausted, or the
deadline. The deadline came first.
That is the schema’s `timebox`: “the declared bound expired first”.
The regime’s rule that unrun states are reported as not run makes the partial answer
reportable. It does not change why the round ended, and u29 falls under neither rule.
It was run by the deadline and undecided at the verdict.
`timebox` is factually right.

What follows:

- **The record must name where a successor resumes.** At the verdict that was u29’s
  verification and admission, since done in `51097f72e`, and u31 (mask 6015871, stratum
  c4/i>=5/d>=8, a second draw), which remains.
  u1’s and u15’s saved nodes would at most certify round-cap stalls on `--check-saved`
  (F7).
- **The decision.** In this record `timebox` has only ever been paired with `unresolved`
  (22 rounds) or `abandoned` (4), and `accepted` only with `criterion` (62, exp-257
  included). `accepted` with `timebox` would be a first.
  It remains defensible here: H-275 registers no accept rule, its regime pre-declares
  deadline truncation, and the two undecided draws cannot move the counted answer
  outside 24 to 26 closures of 29. The verdict reason should then say that the round
  stopped at the clock with two draws undecided.
  If the coordinator prefers the record’s existing pairings, `unresolved` with the same
  reason is the alternative.
  Either way the figures stand.
- The ledger’s effort column and SYNOPSIS’s effort row follow on `packing-ledger
  render`.

### F2: The Closure Medians Include the Distance-2 Draw (Correction)

The verdict reason gives the closure costs right after the counted fraction: “A closure
cost 550 to 1,972 s of process CPU (median 1,090) and its verification 257 to 954 s
(median 472)”. Those medians are over all 25 closures; 1,090.0 s is u20’s CPU, the 13th
of 25. Over the 24 counted closures, without u8, the CPU median is 1,112.7 s and the
verification median 482.7 s. The ranges do not change, because u8’s 572 s and 277 s lie
inside them. The record reports the distance-2 draws apart everywhere else, as H-275’s
regime requires. Either the medians should read 1,113 and 483 s, or the sentence should
say they include u8.

### F3: The Round’s Wall Stops Before Its Last Draw (Correction)

`effort.wall_seconds: 31588` and “over 31,588 s on two workers from 21:51 UTC” run from
the first start (21:51:23) to u30’s verification (06:37:51). u29 was then still running
on the other worker.
It closed at 07:06:36, and the verdict was written at 07:17:29. The review of exp-252
(its F3) set the precedent: the round’s wall runs to the last draw’s receipt, not to an
earlier milestone. Here that is 33,313 s to u29’s receipt, or 33,966 s to the verdict.
The 29-receipt totals (47,492 s wall, 43,806 s CPU) are right as labelled, but the round
also spent u29’s 4,644 s of wall and 4,522 s of CPU, and 1,899 s of verification after
the verdict.

### F4: u29’s Cost Is Missing From the Cost Answer (Disclosure)

H-275 asks the per-state cost.
u29 is the most expensive closure the round produced: 4,644 s of wall, 4,522 s of
process CPU (producer 2,574 s, checker 2,069 s of wall), 337 steps, 22,720 rows in 20
rounds. The verdict gives its wall only.
It leaves its CPU out of the closure range because u29 was not verified by then, which
is the right rule for counting.
The record should still state u29’s CPU and say that, if u29 is admitted, the top of the
closure range more than doubles, from 1,972 to 4,522 s. At `a0ff1cd9f` its 4,644 s
figure cited `kernel-u29-bc428.json`, which existed only in the scratchpad (`s182/U/`).
`51097f72e` has since committed the receipt and added u29’s result with its CPU, which
settles the receipt and the per-draw figure.
The verdict still gives the cost answer without u29 and says its figures are unchanged.
That is a fair reading of the counting rule, but the verdict should say in one sentence
what the closure range and fraction become with u29: 550 to 4,522 s and 25 of 28.

### F5: u31 Was Stopped by a Rule the Regime Does Not Contain (Disclosure)

H-275’s regime runs the frozen order “until the run operator’s deadline at
2026-10-06T07:14:04Z”, and `queue-K7.sh` checks exactly that deadline before each start.
The stop that kept u31 from running is `stop-k7-at-0617.sh`, written by the coordinator
at about 04:2xZ: no start whose 7,000 s ceiling would end after the session’s lease
expiry, 08:14:04Z, so no start after 06:17. Under the registered deadline the queue
would have started u31 at 06:37:51, when u30’s verification freed a worker, 36 minutes
before 07:14:04. The rule is a sound operational choice, since a run started then could
outlive the session.
It is still a deviation from the registered regime.
exp-257 calls it “the coordinator’s re-plan of future slices” and names the session
deadline, but not that it overrode the regime’s deadline.
“No draw started after 06:17 UTC” is also true of the 23 minutes before it: the last
start was u30 at 05:53:37, and no worker was free between then and 06:17. The record
should say that new starts stopped at 06:17, 57 minutes before the registered deadline,
by the coordinator’s session-lease rule, and that u31 would otherwise have started at
06:37:51.

### F6: The Producer and Checker Seconds Are Wall Time (Correction)

Each closure’s `checked_by` reads “N s of wall and M s of process CPU (producer P s,
checker Q s)”. `producer_seconds` and `checker_seconds` are monotonic phase durations
(`check_n17_subpattern.py`, lines 605 and 606), so P plus Q is the wall, not the CPU.
u24 shows it: 651 plus 728 is 1,379 s against 1,381 s of wall and 939 s of CPU. The
parenthesis belongs after the wall figure.
H-275 asks for the producer, checker and verifier cost separately.
The record gives the split in wall seconds, the CPU only combined, and the verifier’s
cost in wall seconds (`perf_counter`). Saying which is which is enough.

### F7: u1 and u15 Reached the Round Cap Before the Checker’s Ceiling (Observation)

An INCOMPLETE receipt records only status, reason, provenance and times: no cells, mask,
recipe or producer outcome.
So the committed receipts for u1 and u15 cannot by themselves say which state they ran
or what the producer did.
The progress logs (`U-1-bc428.err`, `U-15-bc428.err`) can.
Each holds 408 steps, 24 rounds of the 17 owners, with no closure: a closing step logs
`live_rows: 0`, and their last steps log 64. Both producers ended at the 24-round cap,
`round_cap` (u1 after 3,828 s, u15 after 3,350 s), and the checker then hit the 7,000 s
ceiling during the replay (“capture row wall ceiling”, raised in
`hull_kernel/collision.py`). exp-257’s “neither a closure nor a fixed point” is right.
It could add that no closure was pending: a longer checker ceiling would at most have
certified a round-cap stall, like the endpoint-state control’s.

The same gap affects the queue.
`on_nonclosure` sends an INCOMPLETE run to `CHECK-PENDING` only when its receipt says
`producer_outcome: closed`, a field an INCOMPLETE receipt never carries, so a closed
producer cut short in its check would be logged as a stall.
That did not happen in this round.
It would matter to any later round that reads `stalls-bc428.txt` as non-closures.

### F8: u28 and the Frame (Observation)

u28’s result says its states “were already excluded by earlier admitted entries”.
It is one entry, SW9, admitted in exp-250 a day before the draw.
The frame is built from the selector’s arity-at-most-8 flags, not from the certified
census, so it holds states that admitted certificates of higher arity already exclude.
At the registration, 53 of the tail’s 827 orbits were already certified excluded, and 77
are after the round.
H-275 asks about the frame’s strata, so this changes no figure.
The body’s “Each closed state removes only its own orbit” should read “at most its own
orbit”, and the census decrements describe the certified residue, not the frame.
exp-252’s review found the same overlap for state 5500414 and SW9.

### F9: The Certificate Objects Are Not Hosted (Coverage)

The release `data/n17-x048-session-168-certificates-v1` lists no assets
(`gh release view`, checked at 07:35 UTC). The manifest lists the BC-428 objects, and
the u19 and u23 copies hash to its entries, but the 25 certificates’ seeds and nodes
exist only in the run worktree’s ignored certificates folder in this container.
The census, which rests on committed receipts, does not need them; any replay outside
this container does.

### F10: Loose Wording (Observation)

- “Accepted means the frozen measurement ran”: 29 of its 31 draws ran to a verdict.
- The title’s “each closure admitted on the standing verifier’s full pass”: u29’s
  closure was not, at the verdict (it is at `51097f72e`).
- “Every one of the 21 strata has a finished draw (21/21)”: true, but two of the 21 are
  reached only by the distance-2 draws the record reports apart, one of them INCOMPLETE.
  Of the 19 counted strata, all 19 have a finished counted draw and 17 a verified
  closure.
- “Each closed state removes only its own orbit” (F8).
- The verdict commit’s subject, “BC-428 complete”, outside exp-257: u29 was verifying
  and u31 unrun.

## Proposed Corrections to exp-257

This review does not edit exp-257. The changes it supports:

- `effort.stopped_by: criterion` becomes `timebox`. The verdict reason adds that the
  round stopped at the clock with u29 in verification and u31 unrun, and names those as
  the successor’s resume points.
  The decision stays `accepted` with that sentence, or becomes `unresolved` with the
  same reason, at the coordinator’s choice (F1).
- The closure medians read 1,113 s of CPU and 483 s of verification over the 24 counted
  closures, or the sentence says the medians include u8 (F2).
- `effort.wall_seconds: 31588` becomes 33,966, to the verdict, or 33,313, to u29’s
  receipt, and the totals sentence notes u29’s 4,644 s of wall and 4,522 s of CPU

- The u31 bullet says that new starts stopped at 06:17 under the coordinator’s
  session-lease rule, 57 minutes before the registered deadline, and that u31 would
  otherwise have started at 06:37:51 (F5).
- Each closure’s parenthesis moves after its wall figure (F6).
- u28’s result names SW9; “only its own orbit” becomes “at most its own orbit” (F8).
- With u29 admitted in `51097f72e`, the verdict says what the fraction and the cost
