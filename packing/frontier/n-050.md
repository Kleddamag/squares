# `s(50)` — open

**External intake, 2026-09-28.** wand125’s
[point-and-mixed source](../resources/web/wand125-point-and-mixed-2026-09-28/README.md)
reports `s(50) >= 37/5 = 7.4`, from a `D4`-expanded density of 553 rectangle orbits with
total mass $4999999/100000 < 50$, core side $9977/10000$ and a 201-node angle net,
accepted there by a research copy of Tokoharu’s interval checker that accepts coverage
at least 1 instead of $10001/10000$ and checks each net node’s unit-square centre
domain. It exceeds Green’s reported bound by more than $0.0825$, and the
[review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-09-28-wand125-n50-mixed-verifier.md) of
28 September found both changes to Tokoharu’s checker legitimate and no blocking
finding. This repository’s exact audit checks the premises, and four of the 201
directions replayed here equal the shipped records; the complete coverage replay has not
yet finished here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
wand125’s README says parts of the work were produced with AI assistance under human
direction.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(50) \le 7.57142858$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $7.082762$ from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of $0.4887$. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $8$, which is **larger** than the best known
$7.57142857142857$ two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of $s(50)$: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound $\lceil\sqrt{50}\rceil = 8$ — and it trails the reported construction by
$0.42857142857143$.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of $s(50)$. $s(50)$ is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by Thomas Schadt in 2025, via simulated annealing.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.

Before the 2026-09-28 intake the reported lower bound was the expression
`3*sqrt(14)/25 + 2*sqrt(2) + 101/25` (approximately $7.317426011159$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=7, reports this bound at n=50; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered, and the report stays as
evidence. The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
