# `s(141)` — open

**External intake, 2026-10-01.** Evan Daniel’s
[source](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-01/README.md) reports
$s(141) = 12$ as the case $k = 12$ of $s(k^2 - 3) = k$ for every integer $k \ge 6$
(T-064). By the source’s report, its Lean development derives the family from one finite
covering statement, `Valid7`, which a single exact Python checker at the source decides.
Neither has been run here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged and the case stays
open. The source’s `CREDITS.md` says the work was produced by Claude (Anthropic) in a
single session under human direction.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(141) \le 12$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $11.912712$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving a gap
of $0.0873$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 141$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{141}\rceil = 12$ grid, so the grid is still the best
known packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
