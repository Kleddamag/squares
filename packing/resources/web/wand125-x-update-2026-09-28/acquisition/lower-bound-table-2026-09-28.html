For each number of unit squares from 1 to 100, the strongest lower bound anyone has proved, who proved it, and when. Thirty-five of these values are settled exactly; the rest are still open.

1枚から100枚まで、それぞれについて誰かが証明した最も強い下界と、それが誰のいつの成果かを並べます。35件は値が厳密に確定しており、残りは未解決です。

By whose work誰の成果か

One name accounts for nearly half the table: Nagamochi's 2005 closed form applies to every n and is still the strongest thing known for twenty-one of them. Another ten need no theorem at all — when n is a perfect square the area of the squares already forces the answer, and two more follow from a single observation about the centre of the box. The interesting rows are the ones in between.

表の半分近くを一つの名前が占めます。永持仁の2005年の閉じた式はすべての n に適用でき、21件で今なお最良です。一方、10件は定理を必要としません。n が平方数のときは正方形の面積だけで答えが決まり、さらに2件は箱の中心についての一つの観察から従います。興味深いのはその中間の行です。

Two separate 2026 efforts appear here besides this one. jlevy/squares proves its bounds with the same kind of point-mass certificate; tokoharu replaces the points with uniform densities on axis-aligned rectangles, which is what lets n = 26 and n = 29 go past the point-mass certificates in this work. Kleddamag generalizes the point weights again, to threshold charges: a charge applies when a core captures at least k of m listed sites, with budget ⌊m/k⌋ across disjoint cores. That is what carries n = 11 past jlevy's own certificates. Guzhou0806 combines both languages — point charges together with two-of-three threshold charges in one certificate — and has taken n = 17 past Kleddamag's own value twice in two days. Kleddamag took it back on 26 September with 4.640020, adding rules that charge pairwise-intersecting subsets of sites. evand (the s12/ directory of evand/square-packing, 23 September) proves s(12) ≥ 15680/3951 = 3.968616 with 1,736 weighted points, past jlevy's 3.96; it is not yet in the register and has not been re-run here. Forty-nine rows are marked this work + tokoharu: certificates built here with tokoharu's solver and verifier. For n = 29 they climb from his own certificate at 5.71 to 5.7775, one certified side at a time, and n = 30 and 31 follow by monotonicity. n = 32 starts from our own n = 29 rectangles and n = 45 from our own n = 39 point certificate. The others start from our own point certificates or from a neighbouring rectangle certificate; each row's previous holder is in the last column. These certificates are published in wand125/square-packing-bounds. All of these certificates have been re-checked here against an independent verifier.

The best packing column is the best construction anyone has reported, which is the figure that matters for how much room is left. The repository also carries a second, stricter number per case — only what its own checker has re-derived — and for seventeen cases that falls back to the trivial grid packing of side ⌈√n⌉. Those are the cases where a good packing exists but has not been machine-checked, not cases where nothing better is known.

最良の詰め方の列は、誰かが報告した最良の構成です。どれだけ余地が残っているかを見るにはこの値が要ります。リポジトリにはもう一つ、自前の検証器が再導出できたものだけを載せた厳しい数値もありますが、17件ではそれが一辺 ⌈√n⌉ の自明な格子詰めに落ちます。これは良い詰め方が知られていないのではなく、機械検証が済んでいないという意味です。

Bounds and attributions are read from the case records in the jlevy/squares repository, at its state of 25 September 2026 (commit db3f5f3), with the certificates published in wand125/square-packing-bounds on 26 September added. Where a bound follows from a smaller case by monotonicity — a box that will not hold n squares will not hold more — the record carries the source of the original. The zero rows marked this work come from the certificates in wand125/square-packing-bounds (n = 52 and 68 by the total mass of the n = 53 and 69 certificates, as the register records); the forty-nine marked this work + tokoharu were built with the solver in tokoharu/square-packing-density-bounds and are published in wand125/square-packing-bounds. The Kleddamag rows come from Kleddamag/11-squares-certified-bound and Kleddamag/17-squares-certified-bound; both were re-run here and reproduced their published results exactly. The Guzhou0806 row comes from Guzhou0806/n17-square-packing (result R052, 462003/100000) and has not been re-run here.
