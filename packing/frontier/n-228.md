# `s(228)` — open

Open. The best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s (T-056), gives
`s(228) ≤ 15.604638007678682`, and the strongest lower bound independently verified here
is `15.106735` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4979`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Francisco Couzo’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md)
reports a packing of side `15.604638007678682` for this count, dated 27 September 2026
and unchanged at the pinned revision `f3c5a52` of 27 September 2026 (T-056). Its first
packing for this count, of side `15.608976525083040`, is dated 24 September 2026 at
10:28 UTC by the author’s clock.
The repository names no method and no tolerance, and itself states no AI assistance; its
author said on [issue #227](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/227) that he found
the 102 and 103 packings “with the help of Claude”.

This repository certifies it exactly.
The retained decimal pose rounds to an exact rational packing at centre dilation 1, of
side `15.6046380076786828209…`, `8.209e-16` above the printed side, and every pair and

([receipt](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md#certified-here)).
That proves `s(228) ≤ 15.604638007678683`, the verified upper bound; it says nothing
about optimality.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for `s(228)` is
`7*sqrt(30)/113 + 2*sqrt(2) + 1357/113` (approximately `15.17657384178`). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=15, reports this bound at n=226; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
Inherited at n=228 by monotonicity.
This is a source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified bound above.
The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
