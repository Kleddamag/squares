## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $9$, which is **larger** than the best known
$8.88166675700900$ two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of $s(70)$: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound $\lceil\sqrt{70}\rceil = 9$ — and it trails the reported construction by
$0.11833324299100$.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of $s(70)$. $s(70)$ is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by Joe DeVincentis in 2014, via a hand construction.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **4** over $\mathbb{Q}$. Being a quartic it *is*
solvable in radicals — the Galois group is `S4` — so a closed form exists in principle.
It is not recorded because the radical expansion runs to many lines and carries no
insight the quartic does not already carry; the empty `exact_form` here means
“impractical”, not “impossible”, which is the opposite of the situation at $n = 28$ and
$n = 39$.

## The lower bound

The exact replay verifies all five covering-certificate conditions over the complete
rational direction net.
A second interval implementation or generalized native importer would add confirmation
and reuse, but it is not a missing premise of this fixed bound.

Nagamochi’s earlier general closed form remains part of the evidence history and applies
to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```
