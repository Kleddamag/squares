H-267’s `criterion.direction`, as registered:

> Confirm if the certified residue is at most 10^4 orbits with every certificate
> independently checked; reject if it exceeds 10^4 at arity seven, or if any certified
> pattern excludes the endpoint state.

arity at most seven … exclude by containment all but at most 10^4 of the D4 orbits of
closed capacity-one assignments, and never exclude the endpoint’s state”, on the H-266
cover, at cap 1169/250. `threshold: 10000`.

exp-251 tests that exactly:

- **Population.** The census counts the 17-cell subsets of the 24 cells of
  `ring-3-voronoi-8-tabbed-unique` (346,104 states in 43,593 orbits, H-266’s figures,
  which the census refuses to run without).
  This is the cover H-266 accepted in exp-247.
- **Arity.** A sub-pattern is a set of occupied cells, and its arity is the number of
  cells, as in the selector’s `arity` field.
  The filter `len(e['cells']) <= 7` keeps W7 (7), A (6), `s182-k1` and `s182-k3` to
  `s182-k9` (7 each) and `s182-bc427-t4` (7). It drops SW9 (9), the ten BC-425 entries
  (8 each), and N1 with the eight whole-state entries (17 each).
  The census refuses repeated cells, so `len` is the arity.
- **Direction.** “At most”: 9,990 ≤ 10,000.
- **Census definition.** The exact count of D4 orbits with no D4 image of any admitted
  sub-pattern, which is H-267’s metric.
  exp-251 reads it at arity seven only, as the reject clause requires, not on the full
  census of record (4,874 orbits), which would have passed much earlier.

adapted kernel. exp-251 does not mention it (F5).
