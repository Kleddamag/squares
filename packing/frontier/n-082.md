# `s(82)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives $s(82) \le 9.53553391$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $9.058621$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving a gap
of $0.4769$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The packing

Found by Frits Göbel in 1979, via a hand construction: the $n = 65$ centred-block family
pose with one L of seventeen unit squares around it, exactly as `[Friedman DS7]` section
2 states. `cases/gobel82` builds it and verifies it exactly over `Q(sqrt 2)`, which is
what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto `6 + (5/2)sqrt(2)`. The
retained witness declares that side — the exact value correctly rounded up at its
thirty-two digits — but a different layout: its centre set matches none of the
construction’s eight dihedral images, so the certificate is about the construction, not
the witness’s drawing.

## The lower bound

The selected external report, [Friedman DS7], gives the lower-bound expression
`94*sqrt(2)/41 + 247/41` for $s(82)$ (approximately $9.266733533246$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=9, reports this bound at n=82; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
This changes the reported source field only; the independently verified lower bound
remains $9.0622577483$ (**corrected 2 October 2026:** now $9.058621$, Karakuş’s general
bound; see below).
The [source audit](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py) compares the
exact theorem expressions separately from opaque table decimals.

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
