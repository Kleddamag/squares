## Disposition

**Confirmed with corrections.** The verdict stands.
Two of the four frozen states, 2784767 and 2817021, closed under SW9’s recipe well
inside the 7,000 s ceiling, and the standing kernel verifier re-proved each in full.
Both were admitted only after the endpoint-state control finished without closing, so
the registered threshold of two is met and the falsifier (fewer than two) cannot fire.
The registration was committed and pushed before every run, and the frozen list never
changed.
Every receipt carries the registered recipe exactly, from the clean run worktree
at `cebb5d15a`. Every launch the restarts killed was re-run from scratch.
The census deltas recompute exactly.

The corrections touch neither the verdict nor any number a result rests on:

- **F1:** The mechanism sentence misquotes lane D’s classification.
  The north-wall knot is in 2817021, 3063677 and 2878207, not in “the distance-4
  stalls”. 2784767’s knot is on the west wall, and the margins run 0.011 to 0.018, not to
  0.015.
- **F2:** The procedure is described as run in two places where it was not.
  The control ran beside the first three states, not only the first, and the states ran
  one at a time, not two at a time.
  The control had two earlier launches killed; the first state had one.
  The deviation from H-274’s registered order is not named.
- **F3:** “2878207 closed after the verdict” is imprecise.
  Its producer closed at 16:08:36, before the verdict was fixed at 16:09:56; its
  verification and admission came after.
- **F4:** The table’s N1-recipe figure for the control (333 s) is wall time, while the
  column’s other entries are process CPU; the control’s process CPU was 321 s.

Two items are pending rather than wrong: the round’s wall will need extending when
3063677 ends (F5), and the certificate objects are listed in the hosted manifest but not
yet uploaded (F6). A patch with F1 to F4, `exp-253-corrections.patch`, is filed beside
this review.

H-274 is a `hypothesis`, and its register status reads “needs review” until the operator
clears `needs_review`. Its three admissions are census entries, recomputed below.

## Findings

### F1: Lane D’s Mechanism Is Misquoted (Correction)

exp-253 says “The distance-4 stalls share a north-wall knot whose cut margins, about
0.011 to 0.015, sit below the first-order losses of 32 uniform bins”, citing lane D’s
classification. Both filed versions of that review say otherwise:

- side-N1 and side-N2 on the north wall “in three of them” (2817021 and 3063677 at
  distance 4, 2878207 at distance 6);
- side-W0 and side-W1 on the west wall in 2784767 (distance 4), at medians 0.0168 and
  0.0177;
- margins “0.011 to 0.018”.

The sentence repeats H-274’s registration note, written at 11:42:52, ten minutes before
lane D first filed. H-274’s text stays as registered; the mechanism is not part of its
criterion. exp-253 cites the filed review, so its paraphrase should match it.

### F2: The Procedure as Run (Correction)

- `method.control` says the control “ran beside the first state”, and the body says “The
  control and the first state ran side by side”.
  The control ran 6,601 s, beside 2784767, 2817021 and 2878207 in turn.
- `method.operator` says the queue ran “the states two at a time in mask order”.
  That was the design (`queue-M.sh`: “then the rest two at a time”), but the control
  held the second slot until 16:09:56, when only 3063677 was left.
  No two state producers ever overlapped.
- “Two earlier launches of the control and the first state were killed” reads as two of
  each. The control was killed twice (launched 12:07 and 13:29); 2784767 once (13:29).
- The body says Session 182 “changed to that procedure” without naming what it changed
  from. H-274’s regime ran the control first.
  The change keeps the registered voiding rule (see “Registration and Timeline”), and
  naming the registered order and `4a49f464b` makes the deviation explicit.

### F3: 2878207’s Timing (Correction)

The verdict reason says “The third, 2878207, closed after the verdict too”, and the body
says it “closed after the verdict in 875 s of CPU”. Its producer closed at 16:08:36,
before the control ended (16:09:56) and before the verdict commit (16:14:27). Its
verification (16:15:37) and admission (16:16:40) came after.
The table’s “admitted after the verdict” is right.
The session record’s “State 2878207 closed after it” has the same imprecision; it is
outside the patch.

### F4: The Control’s N1-Recipe Figure (Correction)

The table’s “Under N1’s recipe” column gives process CPU for the three states (828, 828
and 925 s, as the results text says) but 333 s for the control.
That is the control’s wall time (333.6 s); its process CPU was 321.1 s.

### F5: The Round’s Wall While 3063677 Runs (Pending)

`effort.wall_seconds: 6601` and the prose bound it from the counted launch to the
control’s end. That is accurate as stated, and `stopped_by: criterion` is correct.
The record already includes 2878207, whose verification ended at 16:15:37, and says
3063677 “runs on for the cost”.
When 3063677 ends, the wall and that sentence need the same update exp-252’s F3 made,
and its result row joins the record.
The killed launches ran about 38 minutes of wall for the control (12:07 to 12:45), plus
about 7 minutes each for the control and 2784767 (13:29 to 13:36); their CPU is
unrecorded. exp-253 says they are not counted, and the effort excludes them.

### F6: The Certificate Objects Are Not Yet Hosted (Coverage)

The six objects are listed in the hosted manifest with sizes and SHA-256 that match the
run worktree’s files.
The release `data/n17-x048-session-168-certificates-v1` lists no assets (`gh api`,
checked at 16:34 UTC), and each admission commit says “not yet uploaded”.
Until they are uploaded, the only copies are in this container’s scratchpad, which has
been through three restarts today.
exp-253 says only that the objects are “in the hosted-data manifest”, which is true.

### F7: Smaller Observations

- The round does not test lane D’s mechanism.
  SW9’s recipe changes the base bins (32 to 64), the producer share (0.5 to 0.6) and
  adaptive rows at once, and no closure’s rows went finer than 1/128. exp-253 notes the
  1/128 point and claims no confirmation of the mechanism, so nothing needs correcting.
- The control passed at its round cap 401 s short of the 7,000 s ceiling.
  An `INCOMPLETE` control would have voided both closures, as BC-423’s did.
- The ledger and SYNOPSIS rows exp-253 feeds carry the operator text F2 corrects; they
  follow on `packing-ledger render`.
