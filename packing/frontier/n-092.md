# `s(92)` — open

**External intake, 2026-10-01.** wand125’s
[mixed-certificate source](../resources/web/wand125-point-and-mixed-2026-10-01/README.md)
reports $s(92) \ge 969/100 = 9.69$ (T-069), from a density of 832 rectangles of total
mass $9199999/100000 < 92$, accepted there at every net angle by its research copy of
Tokoharu’s checker at threshold one.
The complete replay has not run here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
wand125’s README says parts of the work were produced with AI assistance under human
direction.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(92) \le 10$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $9.660254$ from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of $0.3397$. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 92$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{92}\rceil = 10$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
