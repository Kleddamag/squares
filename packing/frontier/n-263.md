# `s(263)` — open

Open. The best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s (T-056), gives
$s(263) \le 16.742280159187313$, and the strongest lower bound independently verified
here is $16.231546$ from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of $0.5107$. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Francisco Couzo’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md)
reports a packing of side $16.742280159187313$ for this count, dated 27 September 2026
and unchanged at the pinned revision `f3c5a52` of 27 September 2026 (T-056). Its first
packing for this count, of side $16.742448478819039$, is dated 24 September 2026 at
10:28 UTC by the author’s clock.
The repository names no method and no tolerance, and itself states no AI assistance; its
author said on [issue #227](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/227) that he found
the 102 and 103 packings “with the help of Claude”.

This repository certifies it exactly.
The retained decimal pose rounds to an exact rational packing at centre dilation 1, of
side $16.7422801591873134267\ldots$, `4.267e-16` above the printed side, and every pair
and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by the promotion’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it
([receipt](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md#certified-here)).
That proves $s(263) \le 16.742280159187314$, the verified upper bound; it says nothing
about optimality.

### The previous best known packing

Before this intake the best known packing was the Kingbird catalogue’s, of side

Found by David Ellsworth in 2025, via an unrecorded method.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
