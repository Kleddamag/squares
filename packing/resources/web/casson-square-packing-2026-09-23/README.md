This packet retains
[griffcass/square-packing](https://github.com/griffcass/square-packing), Griffin
Casson’s 39 packings of unit squares in a square for `n = 103…307`, each smaller than the
Kingbird catalogue as it stood on 23 September 2026. Its Frontier key is
**[griffcass square-packing 2026-09-23]**.

Every one of the 39 is larger than Francisco Couzo’s packing at the same count, retained
in the [sibling packet](../franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md), so none of
them holds a field in the Frontier records and none was replayed here.
They are kept because they are a parallel result at the same counts, published within a
few days of Couzo’s, and the record states priority rather than inferring it:
`source-coverage.yaml` lists each as a superseded report, and each case record states
both sources’ dates and values in its body and its `priority_notes`.

## The Claims

Each packing file gives the side on its first line and one line per square with its
centre and angle, origin at the container’s centre, in the catalogue’s format.
The README says two packings (`n = 106, 123`) are new arrangements found by GPU simulated
annealing followed by sequential linear programming, and the other 37 are SLP polishes of
the catalogue’s own records.
Every packing has at least `1e-10` clearance between squares and `5e-12` from the walls,
passes Ellsworth’s `check_packing.py` at 16 digits and the author’s 50-digit
separating-axis checker `verify_mp.py`, and fails that checker with its side shrunk by
`2e-11`. The retained `results/summary.csv` records those three outcomes per packing.
None of this was replayed here.
