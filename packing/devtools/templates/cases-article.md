# Case Records

<p class="subtitle">Every tracked case, <var>n</var> = 1 to {{LAST_N}}, one record each</p>

</div>

Each record gathers what the project knows about packing $n$ unit squares in the
smallest square: the best packing known, drawn from its atlas rendering; every bound,
reported and verified, with its exact form or minimal polynomial and its credit; every
result in the register that concerns the case, with its rungs; and the case file’s own
account.
All {{COUNT}} are read from their records, `packing/frontier/n-NNN.md`, when the
page is built.
A case in the [frontier atlas](frontier.html) or the overview’s atlas grid
opens its record here.
